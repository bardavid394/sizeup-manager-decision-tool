Manager Decision Tool

Use this tool after completing the employee verification call to determine the applicable manager outcome.
Manager Confidence comes first. High and Moderate confidence cases may continue to Severity. Low or Unable to Determine cases require Further Review.

Severity measures the seriousness, urgency, and negotiability of the employee's circumstances. It does not determine whether the request is genuine.

Employee Track Record and Shift Score affect the decision only for Level 3, Level 4, and Level 5 cases.
Determine Manager Confidence based on the employee's submission, verification call, and supporting information available.
Assign Severity based on the employee's confirmed circumstances after the verification call.
Use the baseline Shift Score after removing the requesting employee and before making coverage adjustments.
MANAGER DECISION TOOL OUTCOME
If new information materially changes the employee's circumstances at any point, reassess Manager Confidence and Severity before finalising the case.
